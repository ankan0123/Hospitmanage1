# IRM (Institute of Reproductive Medicine) Website

A clean, responsive, and modern website created in **HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript** replicating the design shown in the provided screenshots.

## 📂 Project Structure

```text
project hospit/
├── index.html           # Home Page ("Building Families Since 1986", Founder, Team, Stats)
├── services.html        # Services & Treatments Page ("Specialized Procedures & Fertility Solutions", FAQs)
├── blog.html            # Insights & Blog Page ("Fertility Insights, Expert Advice & Patient Stories")
├── css/
│   └── style.css        # Clean custom responsive CSS, color variables, typography, layouts
├── js/
│   └── main.js          # Interactive JavaScript (Modal booking popup, FAQ accordion, category filter, search)
└── README.md            # Project documentation & instructions
```

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Pages Included**:
   - **`index.html` (Home)**:
     - Top announcement bar with location and phone contacts
     - Modern navigation bar with active links and **"BOOK APPOINTMENT"** CTA button
     - Hero section with key statistics and cleanroom lab highlight card
     - Late Prof. B. N. Chakravarty Founder tribute banner
     - "From Hope to Parenthood" 5-step patient journey
     - Comprehensive fertility care procedure cards with colored pill tags
     - State-of-the-Art cleanroom laboratory showcase
     - "More Than Treatment: A Partnership for Life" care support cards
     - Expert doctors & reproductive specialists faculty cards
     - Real patient success stories & testimonials
     - High-impact dark teal trust banner and partner/accreditation logos
     - Locations card (Kolkata Main Centre & Siliguri Hub)
     - Interactive consultation booking form with validation
     - Full footer with quick links, treatments, contact info, and newsletter signup
   - **`services.html` (Specialized Procedures & Fertility Solutions)**:
     - 9 comprehensive procedure cards (IVF & ICSI, IUI, Ovulation Induction, Male Infertility & TESA, Fertility Preservation, PGT-A Genetic Testing, Blastocyst Culture, Laparoscopy & Hysteroscopy, Recurrent IVF Failure Clinic)
     - Interactive filter chips to filter procedures by category
     - Science & Precision cleanroom lab details
     - Interactive FAQ Accordion on treatments
   - **`blog.html` (Fertility Insights & Patient Stories)**:
     - Knowledge hub hero with live search filter
     - Category filter chips (IVF, Lifestyle, PCOS, Stories, Advice, Research)
     - Featured spotlight story card with podcast audio tag and author doctor info
     - 6 curated article cards with tags, read times, and author credentials
     - Numbered pagination controls
     - Doctor Q&A anonymous report second-opinion review callout banner
     - 4 condition pathway cards (PCOS, Male Factor, Failed IVF, Cross-Border)
     - IRM Fertility & Science Digest newsletter box
2. **Interactive JavaScript Components**:
   - **Appointment Booking Modal**: Clicking any "BOOK APPOINTMENT" or "Book Consultation" button triggers a popup modal form with seamless backdrop dismiss.
   - **Treatment & Blog Category Filter**: Live filtering of cards without page reloads.
   - **Live Search**: Instant keyword filtering across blog article titles and descriptions.
   - **Accordion FAQs**: Collapsible frequently asked questions.
   - **Mobile Friendly**: Responsive navigation menu toggle for mobile and tablet screens.
   - **Floating Quick Contact Dock**: Fixed call, WhatsApp, and inquiry buttons on the right side of the screen.

## 🚀 How to Run / View

Simply double-click or open any of the HTML files in your favorite browser:
- Double click **`index.html`** to start on the Home page.
- You can navigate smoothly across **Home**, **Services**, and **Blog** using the header navigation links.
